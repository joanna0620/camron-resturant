Privacy Policy.

Last updated · August 2026

The Camron Restaurant & Bar respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use this website, make an enquiry and interact with links to third-party services.

1. Information We May Receive

When you contact the restaurant through the website, we may receive information you choose to provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, preferred date, number of guests and enquiry details.

2. How We Use Information

Information submitted through the website may be used to respond to enquiries, assist with reservations, answer questions and provide requested restaurant information.

3. WhatsApp Enquiries

The enquiry form can open WhatsApp with the details you entered. WhatsApp is a third-party service and handles information according to its own privacy practices. Please review WhatsApp's policies before using the service.

4. Google Maps & External Services

The website uses or links to services such as Google Maps, Google reviews, Instagram and other external platforms. These services may process information according to their own policies. External links are provided for convenience and navigation.

5. Cookies & Technical Data

The website may use standard browser or technical functionality needed for navigation, responsiveness and performance. If analytics, advertising or additional tracking technologies are introduced in the future, this policy may be updated accordingly.

6. Data Security

Reasonable care is taken to protect information submitted through the website. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices

You may choose not to provide optional information. If you have submitted an enquiry and want to ask about the information associated with it, contact the restaurant directly using the details on the website.

8. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, services or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with the updated date shown above.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact The Camron Restaurant & Bar directly through the contact details provided on the website.
