01 / ABOUT

A table made for
slow afternoons.

The Camron Restaurant & Bar brings together the flavours of Goa, seafood favourites and a relaxed bar atmosphere in Majorda, South Goa.

Come hungry, stay a little longer. Whether it is lunch with friends, a family dinner or drinks after sunset, the mood is easy and the table is yours.
